What it does

Inventory that adds up

Spool catalogue with material, colour and remaining weight. Barcode scanning, automatic write-off as jobs finish, low-stock alerts before you run out mid-print.

Orders and real cost

Filament, electricity, depreciation, labour and packaging in one calculation. Target price and margin. PDF invoices with your own business details.

Printers in one place

Klipper/Moonraker, Duet and Bambu Lab — the latter over LAN, no cloud account required. Live status, G-code console, interactive calibration macros.

Fits your workflow

Telegram, Discord and webhook notifications. Spoolman sync. Server mode with a web interface on your own network. CSV and encrypted backups. Seven languages.

See the app screen by screen in the product tour, or read the detailed questions and answers.

Privacy by architecture

There is no SpoolFarm server. The app contains no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising SDKs and no user accounts. We are technically unable to receive your data.

Your spools, orders, customer names and prices stay on your device. Data leaves it only through integrations you configure yourself, and goes only to the address you entered — your printer, your server, your bot.
