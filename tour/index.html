The problem this was written for

With one printer, you keep it all in your head. Once there are several, you lose track of the money.

How much filament is actually left and what runs out next week. What a specific print really cost, counting not just plastic but electricity, printer wear and failed jobs. Whether you made money on an order — or quietly paid for it yourself.

The slicer shows grams. The bank shows a monthly total. Between them there is nothing. Most people plug that gap with a spreadsheet and abandon it within a month, because every print has to be typed in by hand.

SpoolFarm closes the gap by itself: filament is deducted when a print finishes, cost is calculated automatically, and every order shows what you actually earned.

What it looks like

The main screens of the app and what each one is responsible for.

Overview

The whole farm on one screen

Printers of different brands in a single list, not across four apps. You can see at a glance who is printing, who is idle and where something went wrong.

  • Status, progress and nozzle, bed and chamber temperatures
  • Bambu Lab works over the local network, with no cloud account
  • Pause, cancel and jump to controls straight from the tile

Inventory

Every spool tracked to the gram

The remaining weight goes down by itself when a print finishes. You never log consumption by hand — which is exactly where spreadsheets die.

  • Search and filters by material, sorting by remaining weight
  • Warning with a forecast: "TPU, 84 g left — about 10 days"
  • A low-stock threshold on every spool
  • Barcodes and NFC tags, plus a quick stocktake

Spool detail

Everything about a spool in one place

Not just "blue PLA" but the whole story: what is left, where it sits, what it cost and what has already been printed from it.

  • Remaining weight in grams and per cent, plus the spool's own weight
  • Storage location and lot number — so you can find it on the shelf
  • Price per kilo and the value of what is left
  • Print history for this spool with the usage of each job
  • QR code and NFC tag writing

Money

Cost, not grams

The slicer only knows about plastic. Margin is eaten by the lines it cannot see: electricity, printer wear, labour and the share of failed prints.

  • Price a job before you start it, instead of quoting by feel
  • Cost, price and profit shown per item at once
  • Several models in a single estimate
  • Filament costed at the real price of the actual spool

Orders

Profit, not just revenue

The summary at the top covers every order: how many are in progress, what they brought in and what is left of it after cost.

  • Line items, quantity, price, customer
  • Filters by stage: new, in progress, ready, delivered
  • PDF invoices with your own business details
  • Packaging deducted from stock and included in cost

Reports

Production and business, side by side

Two halves of the same week: how much you printed, and how much you made doing it.

  • Completed jobs, failures, success rate
  • Filament used and hours printed
  • Material cost, revenue, cost of sales, profit
  • Pick the period: week, month, 30 days, all time

Analytics

Where the plastic and the money go

See which material drains the budget, how much capital is frozen in stock, and how consumption shifts month to month.

  • Total inventory value as a single figure
  • Material split: PLA, PETG, ASA, ABS, TPU, PA-CF
  • Usage over time
  • Breakdown by printer

Printers

Real control, not just a viewer

Layers, time remaining and the finish time, temperatures, axis movement, lighting and fans — all from the phone.

  • Pause, cancel and skip object
  • Camera streaming
  • G-code console and interactive Klipper macros
  • File queue and sending a model to print
  • Direct phone-to-printer connection, no middleman servers

Packaging

Boxes run out as quietly as filament

Bags, boxes, tape and thank-you cards get their own stock with prices and quantities, deducted when an order is placed and counted into cost.

  • Unit price and current quantity
  • Low-stock warnings
  • Automatic deduction from order line items

Maintenance

The printer reminds you itself

The app counts runtime hours per machine and tells you what is due — before you find out in the middle of a customer's order.

  • Runtime tracked per printer
  • Bed cleaning, extruder inspection, belt tension, Z axis lubrication
  • Overdue work is called out separately
  • Marking a task done resets its counter

These are not all the screens: notification settings, server mode, the G-code console and the filament load wizard did not fit. The full list is below.

How it differs from what already exists

These tools exist separately, and many of them are free. The difference is that none of them answers the question "did this job make money", and none of them keeps printers of different brands in one place.

Privacy is built into the architecture

There is no SpoolFarm server. The app contains no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising SDKs and no user accounts. The developer is technically unable to receive your data.

Spools, orders, customer names and prices stay on the device. Data leaves the phone only through integrations you configure yourself, and only to the address you entered: your printer, your server, your bot. The app also works with no internet at all.

Closed testing

The Google Play release is being prepared and closed testing is running: a limited group gets builds and sends feedback before the public release. All that is asked of a tester is to use the app in real work and say what broke, what feels awkward and what is missing. Several features exist because someone described a workflow the developer had not thought of.
