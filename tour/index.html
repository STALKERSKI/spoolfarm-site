Why this exists

Your slicer shows grams. Your bank shows the monthly total.
Nothing lives in between.

With one printer everything fits in your head. Add a few more and the first thing you lose is the money: how much filament is actually left, what a specific print really cost once electricity and wear are counted, whether you made anything on that order — or quietly paid for part of it yourself.

The usual fix is a spreadsheet, abandoned a month later because every print has to be entered by hand. SpoolFarm closes that gap on its own: it writes filament off when a print finishes, computes the real cost and shows the profit on every order.

What it looks like

The five screens everything else rests on.

Farm overview

The whole farm on one screen

Printers of different brands in a single list instead of four separate apps. You can see at a glance who is printing, who is idle and where something went wrong.

  • Status, progress and temperatures for nozzle, bed and chamber
  • Bambu Lab over your LAN, no cloud account required
  • Pause, cancel and jump into control straight from the tile

Inventory

Every spool, down to the gram

The remaining weight drops by itself when a print completes. Nothing to enter by hand — that is exactly where spreadsheets usually die.

  • A forecast, not just a number: “TPU, 84 g left — about 10 days”
  • Per-spool low-stock threshold
  • Search and filters by material, sorting by what is left
  • Barcodes and NFC tags for fast stocktaking

Money

Cost, not grams

A slicer only knows about plastic. Profit is eaten by the items it never sees: electricity, printer wear, your labour and the share of failed prints.

  • Quote before you print — so you name a price deliberately, not by feel
  • Cost, price and profit per line item, immediately
  • Several models in one calculation
  • Filament priced from the actual spool you will use

Orders

Profit, not just revenue

The summary on top covers every order: how much is in progress, how much it brought in and how much of that survives the cost of making it.

  • Line items, quantity, price, customer
  • Filters by stage: new, in progress, ready, shipped
  • PDF invoice with your own business details
  • Packaging is written off from stock and folded into the cost

Printers

Real control, not just a viewer

Layers, remaining time and finish time, temperatures, axis movement, lighting and part cooling — all from the phone.

  • Pause, cancel and skip object
  • Camera stream
  • G-code console and interactive Klipper macros
  • File queue and sending a model to print
  • A direct phone-to-printer connection, no server in the middle

How it differs from what already exists

The individual tools exist, and many of them are free. The difference is that none of them answers “did I make money on this”, and none keeps printers of different brands in one place.

Privacy is built into the architecture

There is no SpoolFarm server. The app contains no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising SDKs and no user accounts. The developer is technically unable to receive your data.

Spools, orders, customer names and prices stay on the device. Data leaves the phone only through integrations you configure yourself, and goes only to the address you entered: your printer, your server, your bot. The app also works offline.
